FAQ

What are SHNAPS magnets made of?
Each magnet is made with high-quality photo paper and a strong magnetic backing, finished with a matte coating to resist fingerprints and light scratches.
How big is each magnet?
Each magnet measures 5x5cm for the standard size, and 7.5x7.5cm for the jumbo size.
Are the magnets waterproof or smudge-proof?
Yes! All magnets are smudge-proof and water-resistant thanks to their matte finish.
Can I customize the layout or shape of the magnet set?
While we offer fixed layouts, we occasionally open up slots for custom designs — stay tuned on our IG!
What's the difference between The Collection, The Gallery, and The Archive?
The Collection (9pcs), The Gallery (18pcs), and The Archive (48pcs/108pcs) vary in number of magnets and packaging style — The Archive includes a board!
How long does shipping take?
It takes 2-4 working days to ship out orders. Delivery can take another 2-4 days, depending on your location.
Do you ship overseas?
Currently, we ship only within Singapore. Stay tuned for international options!
How much is delivery?
Delivery is FREE with 9 or more pieces. Otherwise, a flat $3 delivery fee applies.
What if my magnets arrive damaged?
Let us know with a photo within 48 hours and we'll send a replacement!
Do you accept refunds?
Due to the custom nature of SHNAPS, we don't offer refunds, but we're happy to assist if anything's wrong.
What's included in The Archive bundle?
A laser-engraved steel board, your first set of magnets, a gift box, and reminders to complete your year.
How do I submit more photos each month?
We'll email or text you every month with a link to upload new images!
What if I forget to upload one month?
No worries — we'll follow up and you can always submit past months if needed.
